Blueberry Butter Swim Biscuits

Hot, fluffy, blueberry butter swim biscuits with a cream cheese glaze that sinks into every golden, butty crevice. Lord have mercy, they are pure heaven!

I thought I didn’t like blueberry butter swim biscuits….

I’ve received so many requests to make the viral blueberry butter swim biscuits. I tried a few recipes and was not impressed. I swear this is the story of my life. Social media hypes up a recipe; I get excited to try it and end up like “meh”.

After reading online reviews, I noticed many people had the same problems with blueberry butter swim biscuits: biscuits that came out undercooked in the center or had a rubbery texture.

It was time to play!

A few test batches later, this blueberry butter swim biscuits recipe checked every box of perfection. They bake up with crisp, golden-brown, buttery edges, a soft, fluffy center, and plenty of hot, jammy blueberries in every bite. 

Don’t Skip The Cream Cheese Glaze

If you’ve wanted to try blueberry butter swim biscuits but worried about how they’d turn out, give this recipe a try! 

Why This Recipe Is A Winner

  • Tested & perfected. I’ve made this blueberry butter swim recipe with varying amounts of blueberries, milk vs. buttermilk, salted vs. unsalted butter, and different baking times. This final recipe is amazing!
  • Easy & quick. This recipe comes together quickly and easily. No rolling pin or biscuit cutter needed like regular biscuits; just plop it in the pan and bake!
  • Loaded with blueberries. This recipe uses whole, fresh blueberries and mashed blueberries to create the best texture, flavor, and beautiful blueberry batter swirls.  
  • Versatile. Perfect for breakfast, brunch, or dessert. So versatile and great with coffee. 

Watch Me Make These Blueberry Butter Swim Biscuits From Start To Finish!

Come into my kitchen and see how I make these blueberry butter swim biscuits step by step! I’ll show you exactly how to mix each ingredient to avoid that gummy texture.

Ingredients You’ll Need

For the Biscuits

  • salted butter, divided (Use a quality brand like Kerrygold)
  • fresh ripe blueberries, divided (remove stems, wash, and dry completely on paper towels).
  • all-purpose flour
  • baking powder
  • salt
  • sugar
  • whole milk

For The Glaze (Don’t skip the glaze)

  • cream cheese, softened
  • powdered sugar, sifted
  • milk
  • vanilla extract
  • salt

How To Make Blueberry Butter Swim Biscuits

1. Preheat the oven to 450 F. Melt 6 tablespoons of the butter in a glass 9×9 baking dish. Set aside.

2. In a food processor, add  1/2 cup of blueberries and blend until pureed. Set aside.

3. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.

4. Grate in the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter into the flour mixture and toss to coat the butter into the flour.

5. Add remaining whole blueberries into the flour mixture and toss to coat.

6. Gently stir in milk just until combined and a sticky dough forms. (Be careful here; over-stirring will create that gummy texture)

7. Drop in spoonfuls of the mashed blueberries and fold gently just to create a few swirls in the batter. (Don’t overdo it, or you’ll have purple-grey biscuits.) (See video for this step)

8. Drop the batter by large spoonfuls evenly into the baking dish right on top of the butter.

9. Smooth it out into an even layer and spread the butter that rises up evenly over the top. Precut the biscuits into nine pieces.

10. Place the baking dish on a baking sheet ( to catch any blueberry juices that might bubble over) and bake for 30-35 minutes. The butter will bubble up around the edges. The biscuits are done when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs clinging to it. (You can cover it loosely with aluminum foil if it starts to brown too much)

11. When the biscuits come out of the oven, poke a few holes in the biscuits and brush with about 1 tablespoon of melted salted butter. (This will allow the glaze to seep down)

11. To make the cream cheese glaze, add the softened cream cheese to a bowl and stir until it is smooth and creamy.  Stir in powdered sugar until thick and crumbly. Add enough milk, a little at a time, until the glaze has a pourable consistency. Stir in vanilla extract and a pinch of salt.

12. Drizzle the glaze over the hot biscuits and allow the glaze to harden before serving. Enjoy!

Mo’s Recipe Tips

Use good-quality salted butter. These biscuits bake in butter, so using a quality butter will pay off big time! I like using Kerrygold brand since it’s easy to find and it really gives these biscuits a beautiful golden color.

Use whole milk. As much as I love buttermilk, whole milk performed better in my test batches and never created that undercooked middle.

Go gentle after adding the milk. To avoid that gummy texture, don’t overwork the dough after adding the milk. I prefer adding the blueberries before adding the milk, although most recipes add it after. Just be very gentle with the dough, and you’ll be fine.

Add a baking sheet underneath the pan. A little of the butter and/or blueberry juice can bubble up and drip over the edge of the baking dish. Adding a pan underneath will catch any drips. (Yep, I smoked up my kitchen a few times by forgetting this)

Use sweet, ripe fresh blueberries. Remember these are biscuits, not muffins or coffee cake, so your blueberries will do a lot of the heavy lifting. Taste your blueberries; if they are bitter, then that’s how they will taste in these biscuits. Use sweet, ripe blueberries.  

Can I use other berries? I have only tested these with blueberries. But I plan to test these with strawberries and blackberries soon and will update.

FAQ’s

Why are they called butter swim biscuits? Butter swim biscuits are a type of biscuit that is baked in melted butter. Instead of adding all the butter to the dough, most of it is melted in the pan, and the dough is placed on top of the melted butter. The dough “swims” in the butter, creating crisp buttery edges.

Why is the center of my blueberry butter swim biscuits Not Done? Blueberries release moisture as they bake, which can cause the center to take longer to cook. Be sure to add no more than 2 cups of blueberries total for this recipe, use the correct pan size, and follow the instructions carefully.  If needed, cover loosely with foil if it starts to brown too much and continue baking in 2-3 minute intervals.

Can I use frozen blueberries? I have only tested this recipe with fresh blueberries.

Can I skip the cream cheese glaze? I don’t recommend it. The sweet glaze adds contrast to the biscuits; otherwise, it’s just a pretty, buttery biscuit with blueberries. That incredible glaze is everything!

Can I use self-rising flour? I don’t see why not! Just skip the salt and baking powder.  However, I’ve only made this recipe with all-purpose flour, so I’m really not sure how self-rising would do. Sometimes self-rising flour can be hit or miss.

Storage

Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days. These do not freeze and reheat well, in my opinion.

Ready To Make These Viral Blueberry Butter Swim Biscuits?

Blueberry Butter Swim Biscuits

5 from 1 rating
Hot, fluffy, buttery blueberry butter swim biscuits with a cream cheese glaze that sinks into every crevice.
Servings: 9
Prep Time: 20 minutes mins
Cook Time: 35 minutes mins
Total Time: 55 minutes mins
Print Recipe Pin Recipe Leave a Review

Ingredients 

  • 2 cups fresh blueberries, divided (washed, stems removed, and completely dried)
  • 8 tablespoons salted butter, divided + more for brushing
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (spooned and leveled off)
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup + 2 Tablespoons whole milk

Cream Cheese Glaze

  • 2 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar sifted
  • 1 tablespoon whole milk
  • ⅛ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • pinch salt

Instructions
 

  • Preheat the oven to 450℉. Melt 6 tablespoons of salted butter in an 8×8 glass baking dish and set aside.
    8 tablespoons salted butter, divided
  • In a food processor or blender, blend ½ cup of blueberries and set aside.
    2 cups fresh blueberries, divided
  • In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
    2 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tablespoon baking powder, ½ teaspoon salt, ½ cup granulated sugar
  • Grate the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter into the flour mixture.
  • Add the remaining whole blueberries (1½ cups)
    and toss to coat.
  • Add the milk and stir very gently until a wet dough forms.
    1 cup + 2 Tablespoons whole milk
  • Drop spoonfuls of the pureed blueberries on the dough and give it a very light stir (See video for demonstration)
  • Drop large spoonfuls of the batter evenly into the prepared pan (right on top of the melted butter.
  • Gently spread into an even layer, and lightly precut the biscuits into nine slices.
  • Place the pan on a baking sheet (in case blueberry juices bubble over the top) and bake for 30-35 minutes. (Cover with foil if the top gets too brown before the inside is done)
  • Remove from the oven and poke a few holes over the surface. (optional). Brush with about 1 tablespoon of melted salted butter.
  • To make the glaze, stir together the softened cream cheese and powdered sugar until crumbly.
    1 cup powdered sugar, 2 oz cream cheese,
  • Stir in the milk a little at a time until you have a smooth, silky, pourable texture.
    1 tablespoon whole milk
  • Stir in the vanilla extract and salt.
    ⅛ teaspoon vanilla extract, pinch salt
  • Immediately drizzle the glaze over the hot biscuits and allow the glaze to set.
  • Serve and enjoy.
