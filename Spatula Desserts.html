23 Dessert Recipes for Pumpkin Spice Fans

If pumpkin is the flavor you wait all year for, this is the time to make the most of it. Fall is when pumpkin desserts make the most sense. These recipes go well beyond the usual pie and give pumpkin fans plenty of reasons to keep baking through the season. The only real problem is deciding which one to make first.

Butterscotch Pumpkin Bundt Cake

This butterscotch pumpkin bundt cake recipe features an airy and super moist pumpkin bundt cake loaded with pumpkin and your favorite fall spices. It is topped with the most delicious butterscotch sauce, which has a lovely buttery, brown sugar flavor profile. Easy and quick candied pecans are added for some extra crunchiness.

Go to the recipe: Butterscotch Pumpkin Bundt Cake

Pumpkin Custard Pie

This is, hands down, the best pumpkin custard pie recipe for fall. It features a flaky, buttery pie crust filled with silky, creamy, incredible, delicious pumpkin custard flavored with aromatic orange zest and nutmeg. This Pumpkin pie is made without evaporated milk, yet super rich, smooth, and full of fall flavors everyone loves!

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Custard Pie

Pumpkin Bread with Cream Cheese Frosting

This incredible, moist, easy pumpkin bread with cream cheese frosting is the perfect fall cake for the pumpkin season. It's a fluffy pumpkin loaf infused with aromatic fall spices, frosted with an easy, three-ingredient cream cheese icing that is very well-balanced in sweetness. It's also made without butter!

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Bread with Cream Cheese Frosting

No-Bake Pumpkin Pie

This no-bake pumpkin pie is rich, creamy, and decadent. It features a two-ingredient crust filled with a perfectly spiced pumpkin puree and cream cheese mixture, resulting in a mousse-like texture. The pie is then topped with mascarpone, making it an irresistible fall or winter dessert.

Go to the recipe: No-Bake Pumpkin Pie

Puff Pastry Pumpkin Turnovers

If you're looking for an easy and quick dessert, then you will love these puff pastry pumpkin turnovers! Crunchy and flaky on the outside and sweet and creamy on the inside, these warm pumpkin treats are super easy to make.

Go to the recipe: Puff Pastry Pumpkin Turnovers

Baked Pumpkin Spice Donuts

You are going to fall in love with these super moist and delicious baked pumpkin spice donuts, which are the perfect start to a perfect morning! They are packed with pumpkin, spiced with all the best iconic fall flavors, and coated in a simple cinnamon sugar mixture that is absolutely yummy!

Go to the recipe: Baked Pumpkin Spice Donuts

Pumpkin Pie with Sweetened Condensed Milk

This delicious Pumpkin Pie with Sweetened Condensed Milk is the best fall dessert we can think of. Made with a super buttery flaky crust, creamy custard-like filling, and yummy mascarpone frosting, this dessert is going to steal the show!

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Pie with Sweetened Condensed Milk

Pumpkin Streusel Bread

When you thought your fall dessert table couldn’t get any better, this moist pumpkin streusel bread steals the show! With a moist pumpkin bread base, a crunchy streusel topping, and a sweet glaze, one slice will certainly not be enough!

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Streusel Bread

Pumpkin Brownies

These gooey pumpkin brownies take the season's best flavors and combine them to create an unforgettable combination! Made with creamy pumpkin puree sandwiched between fudgy brownie layers, they are the perfect fall treat for all ages.

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Brownies

Mini Pumpkin Bundt Cakes with Maple Glaze

These delicious mini pumpkin bundt cakes with maple glaze are the perfect quick and easy individual-size dessert to get your holiday season started this year! Packed with perfectly spiced pumpkin and topped with a divine buttery maple glaze, they are moist, tender, and yummy!

Go to the recipe: Mini Pumpkin Bundt Cakes with Maple Glaze

Pumpkin Banana Muffins

These pumpkin banana muffins are crazy moist, with fragrant fall spices and gooey chocolate chips. The perfect snack or a quick breakfast. The sweet and spicy ‘just baked’ aroma of these muffins will fill your whole house, and you won’t be able to wait before eating them warm and freshly baked straight out of the oven.

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Banana Muffins

Pumpkin Cream Cheese Pie

Pie season is here, and this easy pumpkin cream cheese pie is a dessert dream. Rich and smooth pumpkin cheesecake, blended with the flavors of traditional pumpkin pie. The fall-flavored cream cheese recipe is so delicious, and the contrasting textures make it extra special and indulgent.

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Cream Cheese Pie

Snickerdoodle Pumpkin Cheesecake Cookies

Snickerdoodle pumpkin cheesecake cookies are a must-try this fall season! They are full of your favorite fall flavors and incredibly delicious. The rich pumpkin spice flavor paired with the tanginess of cream cheese makes every bite feel absolutely divine.

Go to the recipe: Snickerdoodle Pumpkin Cheesecake Cookies

Pumpkin Hand Pies

With a creamy pumpkin filling and a buttery, flaky crust, these adorable pumpkin hand pies are a fun and easy addition to your fall dessert table. They are an easy and portable alternative to the traditional pumpkin pie.

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Hand Pies

Pumpkin Blondies

Super gooey and chewy pumpkin blondies loaded with warm pumpkin flavor and spices are the perfect sweet snack for fall! With pumpkin pie spice, chocolate chips, and hints of caramel butterscotch undertones, they might be your favorite easy snack this season!

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Blondies

No-Bake Pumpkin Cheesecake

This smooth, creamy, and tangy pumpkin cheesecake with a crunchy Graham cracker crust and whipped cream cheese decoration is super delicious, but so much easier than it looks! It's perfect for fall and impressive enough even for Thanksgiving, but unbelievably simple!

Go to the recipe: No Bake Pumpkin Cheesecake

Double Layer Pumpkin Pie

With a mousse-like consistency and crunchy graham cracker crust, this no-bake double-layer pumpkin pie will be your go-to dessert this fall. This divine dessert features a homemade biscuit crumb crust filled with layers of rich, light, and airy cream cheese and pumpkin pie fillings, topped with a lightly sweetened cream cheese decoration.

Go to the recipe: Double Layer Pumpkin Pie

Pumpkin S’mores Cookies

There are literally thousands of fantastic cookie recipes to choose from, but if you want to try something different, you can’t go wrong with pumpkin s’mores cookies. They’re soft, sweet, and very more-ish, offering plenty of amazing flavors in every bite! Pumpkin marshmallow cookies are great for any occasion and ideal for pumpkin fans. The combo of pumpkin and s’mores flavors is a magical one!

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin S’mores Cookies

Pumpkin Bundt Cake

This pumpkin bundt cake features all the delicious flavors of fall. With a delightful and beautiful bundt cake packed with flavors like pumpkin, cinnamon, ginger, and cloves, and an easy icing to finish it off, this will be a staple for autumn desserts.

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Bundt Cake

Pumpkin Crumble Bars

It’s hip to be square, which is why these pumpkin crumble bars are the perfect fall dessert. Between the buttery press-in crust, custardy pumpkin pie filling, and nutty crumble topping, these perfectly portioned, pre-sliced treats are like three desserts in one!

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Crumble Bars

Pumpkin Whoopie Pies

The traditional flavor of whoopie pie is chocolate, but we’ve come a long way since then. Pumpkin whoopie pies might be my favorite, especially during the fall season when I get a craving for pumpkin spice everything. All the ingredients for this recipe are readily available at your local grocery store, and you likely already have many of them in your pantry.

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Whoopie Pies

Brown Butter Pumpkin Chocolate Chip Cookies

Bursting with pumpkin flavor, warm spices, nutty brown butter, and chunks of chocolate, these brown butter pumpkin chocolate chip cookies are the only cookie recipe you will need this fall! The texture is perfectly chewy with crispy edges, and the flavors are warm and cozy.

Go to the recipe: Brown Butter Pumpkin Chocolate Chip Cookies

Pumpkin Chiffon Pie

Light, creamy, and perfect for fall gatherings, this pumpkin chiffon pie brings airy elegance to your holiday table. The classic pumpkin flavor is whipped into a silky, fluffy filling that’s set in a crisp graham cracker crust. With pumpkin pie spice and vanilla woven into the mix, it tastes indulgent without feeling heavy. Since it’s chilled instead of baked, it’s a breeze to whip up ahead, and you’ll adore the flavors.

Go to the recipe: Pumpkin Chiffon Pie
