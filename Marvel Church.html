Kyln is the intergalactic inescapable prison for the most dangerous and notorious Marvel villains and at times, heroes. In Marvel Strike Force, Escape From Kyln is the variation of the Avengers Tower Mode where you battle through numerous Floors or Cells in this case. The higher you can get – the more rewards you will earn.

A special feature that distinguishes Escape From Kyln from Avengers Tower or S.W.O.R.D. Satellite is that you can only use 3-character squads in each Cell, due to it being a prison and having cramped and confined spaces, lore-wise. Gameplay-wise, it will be a good challenge and can allow you to stretch your theory-crafting, with some limitations of course. And as always, if for any reason you can’t do it yourself, we are here to provide you with the best character choices for each Cell and advice on how to clear them and get the most out of this new Tower Mode.

Prison Rules

In this version of Kyln, we are going back to using only 3 characters per cell, instead of 5, like in Whiplash Kyln Tower. Now, both characters and teams with the Challengers 3 trait can be used. We will share the exact list of eligible characters a bit later. Raid rules will apply in this Kyln Tower.

There will be 4 difficulty levels to choose from, so players can pick the one that best matches the strength of their roster:

  • Easy: For new users, Level 50 and above
  • Normal: For mid-game players, Level 80
  • Hard: For veteran Commanders, Level 100 and above
  • Omega: For top Commanders with especially powerful rosters

All difficulties are part of the same event, so select the challenge that suits your roster and start your climb!

There will be 20 Cells in total, and the event will last one month (4 weeks). Each week, 5 new Cells will unlock, and at that time, all character cooldowns will reset. When you use a character in a successful battle (meaning you win and clear a Cell), that character goes on cooldown and can’t be used again that week. Additionally, after completing Cell 5, Cell 10, and Cell 15, all cooldowns are reset. Character cooldowns also reset every seven days, as we already mentioned.

This means it’s technically possible to use just 3 teams to clear the first 15 Cells, but for Cells 16 to 20 in the final week, you’ll need 5 full teams of 3 characters each.

Since cooldowns reset every 7 days and after certain Cells, if you don’t have enough eligible characters, you can clear only 3 Cells in the first week with three different teams, then wait for the reset. After that, you can use two of your previously used teams to clear the remaining two Cells, and once you reach Cell 5, all cooldowns will reset again. You can repeat this strategy in the second and third weeks of the event. However, the fourth week will require all 5 teams, because the event ends at the end of the fourth week.

This tactic is useful for players who don’t have enough required characters or teams for this Kyln Tower. But in our walkthrough, you’ll always find guidance for completing all 5 available Cells each week, along with the best tips on which teams and characters to use. This way, you’ll have the optimal strategies to clear every stage as the event progresses.

Rewards

Here’s how powerful you can make Human Torch (Jim) at each Difficulty level, along with the maximum rewards you can earn:

Easy: Up to 3 Yellow Stars and 3 Red Stars
Normal: Up to 5 Yellow Stars and 5 Red Stars
Hard: Up to 6 Yellow Stars and 6 Red Stars
Omega: Up to 7 Yellow Stars, 7 Red Stars, and 2 Diamonds

Choose your difficulty based on how far you want to power up Human Torch (Jim), which rewards you want to earn, and, most importantly, your roster strength.

Walkthrough

Cell 1

Suggested team: Timeless Eternal

For Cell 1, we recommend bringing a full Timeless Eternal team. Many players have heavily invested in this team since they are useful in multiple game modes, so most will have them at high power levels—making them an excellent solution for Cell 1.

You’ll be facing Superior and Sinister Six characters. Right from the start, you have a strong advantage in turn order, as your characters are much faster than the enemies. Focus your attacks on Doctor Octopus first, then take out the remaining Sinister Six members.

In the second wave, Vulture and three Superior Six characters appear. Here, focus on Green Goblin (Classic) first, then Vulture, and finish off the rest. There’s no need for any specific guide for this node, as you’ll consistently have a solid advantage throughout the fight—it’s quite likely the enemies won’t even get a chance to take a turn, or at most, one or two of them may play once.

Cell 2

Suggested team: Storm (Mighty) + Blade (Mighty) + Silver Surfer (Breaker) + Scorpion (Breaker) + Super Skrull

In Cell 2, we brought both Breaker and both Mighty characters, along with Super Skrull. Feel free to use Super Skrull’s Special right at the start of the battle—even though he’ll get Stealth and the enemies will gain Speed Up (since you’re fighting New Mutants), this will immediately fill Super Skrull’s Speed Bar even more. As a result, he’ll be able to play two more times very quickly and use his Ultimate.

After Wolfsbane uses her Special, your other characters will receive a bunch of debuffs, which also helps fill Skrull’s Speed Bar. This guarantees Super Skrull gets three turns at the start, making it easy to use his Ultimate for great damage and to apply nasty debuffs to the enemies.

Once you use Storm (Mighty)’s Ultimate, it’s likely you’ll eliminate at least three enemies, which will trigger the second wave. Just keep up the momentum and finish the battle from there.

In the second wave, you’ll face Mister Sinister, Domino, Cable, and Deadpool. Finish off any remaining New Mutant characters first, then take care of these new enemies.

Cell 3

Suggested team: Insidious Six + Ultron + Vision

Three Insidious Six characters, together with Ultron and Vision, will easily handle this Cell. At the start, it’s important to use Superior Spider-Man’s Special and target Havok, since he’s the fastest enemy. This way, you’ll apply Ability Block to him and to several other opponents.

When it’s Vision’s turn to play, use his Special and target Groot—this will apply Ability Block to Groot, who likely won’t have it yet (since Superior Spider-Man’s Special probably won’t hit him). After that, use Hobgoblin’s Special on Howard by targeting him; after this move, the rest is just execution.

In the second wave, four Winter Guard characters appear, with Red Guardian having Taunt. After using Vision’s Ultimate, you’ll remove his Taunt, allowing you to focus first on Darkstar and Omega Red. Once they’re eliminated, finish off the remaining two Winter Guard enemies.

Cell 4

Suggested team: Immortal Weapon

The full Immortal Weapon team is a more than solid choice for Cell 4. At the start, you’ll need to withstand a few enemy attacks, but Immortal Weapon has excellent mitigation, so this shouldn’t pose a major problem. The only real threat is Satana’s Ultimate, which can (together with other enemies’ attacks) bring Iron Fist dangerously low on HP—but he’ll recover quickly.

With Swordmaster, target Mordo and use his Special. This will strip most enemy buffs and apply Defense Down, Heal Block, and Trauma to everyone, giving you full control of the fight from that point forward. It will also Stun Mordo, so he will not be able to play. It’s a good idea to have all your characters set as Strikers, except for Steel Serpent, who should be a Skirmisher to create a strong Striker-Skirmisher combo—but this isn’t strictly required.

In the second wave, you’ll face three Cabal characters and Silver Samurai. Focus on The Leader until he’s eliminated, then go after the remaining two Cabal members. Silver Samurai will likely fall from the splash damage dealt while attacking The Leader.

Cell 5

Suggested team: Iron Raider

Iron Raider will handle Cell 5 relatively easily. The only annoying character in the first wave might be Forge, who will stay alive a bit longer thanks to his strong sustain—but he’ll go down eventually.

In the second wave, you’ll face Phoenix Force characters, together with Jean Grey and Rachel Summers. You shouldn’t have any trouble dealing with them either. Focus on Magneto first, then Omega Red (Phoenix Force), followed by Jean and Rachel. Leave Phoenix for last.

When played with Raid rules (as in this Kyln Tower), the Iron Raider team is exceptional and capable of overcoming significant obstacles in their path.

Conclusion

The first 5 Cells didn’t pose much of a challenge, but this is just the beginning of Kyln Tower, so we’ll see what awaits us in the coming weeks.

If you’re playing on Omega difficulty, once you complete the first 5 Cells, you’ll recruit Human Torch (Jim) and collect enough Character Shards to have him at 3 Yellow Stars and 3 Red Stars.

If you need any additional help with Kyln, feel free to reach out to us on our Discord. Thank you for your support.
