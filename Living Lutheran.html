Prayer for Oct. 12

Listen to audio version of this article

Every day, Living Lutheran offers a prayer for the day from the ELCA resource “Prayer ventures.” These daily petitions are offered as a guide for your own prayer life as together we pray for the needs of the world and give thanks for the ministries of our church.

You can download a pdf with the monthly “Prayer ventures” here.

Monday, Oct. 12 Indigenous Peoples’ Day  We give thanks for Indigenous communities that have lived in the Americas and other parts of the world for thousands of years. We pray that we will not overlook them in our telling of history and that we will honor our church’s commitment to repentance, reconciliation, healing, respect and unity as children of God.
