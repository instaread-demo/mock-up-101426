Little Girl Gets the Sweetest Surprise After Being Featured in Children’s National Hospital Campaign

The Feels
Share
Suggest an Edit

One little girl got a surprise she will probably remember for a long time.

Her mom, @kiek3093, shared that her daughter was selected to be featured in Children’s National Hospital’s U.S. News campaign, with her photo appearing throughout the hospital and on the organization’s website.

But she did not tell her right away.

Instead, her mom kept the news a secret and brought her to the hospital so she could see the campaign for herself.

“Keeping this a secret was SO HARD,” she wrote. “Seeing her excitement when she realized her face was everywhere made it completely worth it.”

In the video, the little girl sees a large photo of herself displayed on the hospital wall as part of a campaign that reads, “The Only #1 for Your #1.”

The surprise did not stop there.

Her mom said that once they got into the elevator, someone had already recognized her daughter from the photos throughout the hospital.

That made the moment feel even bigger.

“I am beyond proud of my baby,” she wrote. “Seeing her beautiful little face representing Children’s National in this campaign is such an amazing feeling.”

For her daughter, it was a chance to see herself celebrated in a space connected to her own story.

For her mom, it was one of those proud-parent moments that is hard to put into words.

Sometimes the best surprises are the ones you have to work the hardest to keep.
